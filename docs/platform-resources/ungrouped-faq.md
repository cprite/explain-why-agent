# Frequently Asked Questions

**OPEN AGENT HACKATHON 2026 | Build an agent that does real work**

All times are in **UTC**. If anything here conflicts with the Official Rules on the event page, the Official Rules apply.

---

## 1. The Event

**What is the Open Agent Hackathon?**  
A fully online global hackathon where builders create AI agents that do real work: agents that can access relevant information, understand context and relationships, reason across multiple steps, maintain useful context, and turn that understanding into a useful outcome.

**When is it?**  
The build window runs from **22 October, 00:00 UTC** to **27 October 2026, 23:45 UTC**, a total of 144 hours.

**Where does it take place?**  
Fully online. HackOS runs all hackathon operations: announcements, team-finding and team creation, mentor office hours, the #help desk and sponsor Help channels, submission, and the results call.

**What is Discord for?**  
Discord is the GenAI Works community space for community chat and activities after the hackathon: showcases, future events, workshops, and staying connected with other builders.

**Is it a chatbot or prompt-engineering competition?**  
No. The goal is a working agent that does useful work for a real user, industry, or community. Prompt chains, chatbots, and UI-only demos do not meet the bar.

## 2. Registration and Eligibility

**Does it cost anything to enter?**  
No. Registration, the platform workspace, mentor office hours, and submission are all free.

**Who can participate?**  
Anyone aged 16 or older at the start of the build window, anywhere in the world, from students to senior engineers.

**When does registration close?**  
**22 October 2026, 23:45 UTC**. Registration stays open through Day 1 of the build window, and this is a hard deadline.

**How do I register?**  
Sign up on hackathon.genai.works. One sign-up covers the event and community updates.

**Can I create more than one account?**  
No. One account per participant. Duplicate accounts disqualify the whole team.

**Can organizer or sponsor employees take part?**  
Employees of the organizer and of judging sponsors may participate but are not eligible for cash prizes.

---

## 3. Teams

**Can I take part solo?**  
Yes. Select **"happy to build solo"** in the registration form. Solo entries compete in the same pool as teams.

**I do not have a team. Can I still register?**  
Yes. Register, then create a team or join one through team-finding on HackOS, or build solo.

**How many people can be on a team?**  
One to five.

**Can I join more than one team?**  
No. A participant may belong to exactly one team. Mentors may advise any number of teams.

**Can I change my team?**  
Yes, until your team's first submission is created. Before that, you may join, leave or merge teams freely. Team composition locks once the first submission is created.

**How are prizes split within a team?**  
Equally among team members, unless the team submits a different split in writing.

## 4. Tracks

**How many tracks are there?**  
Three challenge tracks plus one bonus track. Every submission selects exactly one primary track, which determines the judging panel and the prizes it competes for.

| # | Track | What You Build |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **Solving Fragmented Intelligence** | Demonstrate a clever ability to discover and work across fragmented data sources. |
| 2 | **The Agent That Can Explain Why** | Build agents that investigate complex questions across multiple data sources, connect findings, and produce evidence-backed answers or recommendations. |
| 3 | **Reasoning Architecture** | Demonstrate a novel ability to turn data, knowledge, memory, and reasoning into reliable decisions and actions. |
| Bonus | **Wildcard [Tinkerer]: Bring Your Own Project** | Bring an existing project, prototype or concept and extend it using Zetaris and Meterless. Judged on the same criteria as the other tracks and scored on the new work rather than prior development. |

**Can I submit to more than one track?**  
No. Each submission selects exactly one primary track. Cross-track relevance is welcome but is not scored twice.

**Can I change track after the build window opens?**  
Yes. You can change your track any time before the submission deadline from your team workspace.

**Which track is easiest?**  
None of the tracks carries a difficulty rating. How ambitious your build is matters more than which track you pick. A focused solution that works beats a broad one that does not.

## 5. Building

**Which models and frameworks can I use?**  
Any language, framework, model or cloud, including closed-source APIs and sponsor credits. Common options are LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen, the OpenAI Agent SDK, LangChain, LlamaIndex, or a custom Python stack. The framework itself is not scored.

**Can I use code I wrote before the hackathon?**  
Work committed before the build window opens is not eligible, except for clearly declared pre-existing components. Pre-existing open-source libraries are fine. Pre-existing product code must be declared in the submission form. The Wildcard [Tinkerer] bonus track is designed for existing projects, and only the new work is scored.

**Can I use AI-generated code?**  
Yes. Generated code is allowed and expected. You own the result and are accountable for it.

**Who are the sponsors, and do I have to use their technology?**  
The sponsors are **Space XAI (with Cursor)**, **NVIDIA**, **Zetaris** and **Meterless**. Some of their technology is required:

| Sponsor | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Zetaris** | Required: every submission must integrate Zetaris |
| **Meterless** | Required: every submission must integrate Meterless |
| **Cursor (with Space XAI)** | Required: teams must use Cursor as their development environment |
| **NVIDIA** | Integrate wherever it contributes to your project, workflow or use case |

**Sponsor Tech is worth 10 points** in the rubric. Each sponsor has a folder in Resources on HackOS with its documentation and resources.

**How do I get access to the sponsor technologies?**  
Access instructions and setup steps are in each sponsor's folder in Resources on HackOS. Resources are being updated as sponsors add material. For help with a sponsor's technology, use that sponsor's Help channel on HackOS (Help Zetaris, Help Meterless, Help Cursor, Help NVIDIA). For general technical questions, use the #help desk channel.

**What data can I use?**  
Open, accessible data you can use legally. Avoid private, proprietary, confidential, or restricted datasets, and avoid real personal data. Keep the repository light by using samples rather than full datasets, and include a fallback mode so your solution still runs if an external API is unavailable. If you prepare data before the build window, declare it as a pre-existing component.

**Should I build a user interface?**  
Only if it makes the output usable. Product & UX is worth 10 points, but a polished interface over a weak system scores badly. The agent doing real work is the point.

## 7. Submission

**When is the submission deadline?**  
**27 October 2026, 23:45 UTC**. This is a hard deadline. Time zones do not extend it.

**Where do I submit?**  
On HackOS.

**What do I need to submit?**

| Component | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Demo video** | 1–4 minutes, showing the main workflow, how the agent solves the selected challenge, its key features, and how Zetaris and Meterless are integrated |
| **Slide deck** | The problem and target users, the solution, the agent workflow or architecture, the technical stack, how Zetaris and Meterless were used, what was unique about your use of the sponsor technologies, product visuals, and future enhancements |
| **GitHub repository** | All source code and a README covering setup, usage and dependencies. If your agents run in Google Colab, include the Colab links |
| **Sponsor technology explanation** | How Zetaris and Meterless were integrated, how Cursor was used during development, where NVIDIA technology contributes, and what was unique about your use of the sponsor technologies |

Declare any pre-existing components in the submission form.

**Can I keep working after the deadline?**  
No. Submit before the deadline and do not rely on post-deadline fixes.

**Can I update my submission before the deadline?**  
Yes, up to the deadline. Remember that your team composition locks when your first submission is created.

**What happens if my submission does not run?**  
A submission a judge cannot run cannot be scored properly. Test a clean setup from scratch before you submit.

**Should I commit my API keys?**  
Never. Keep secrets in a local `.env` file, add it to `.gitignore`, and include a `.env.example` with placeholder values only.

## 8. Judging

**Who judges the submissions?**  
Expert judges review submissions against the published criteria. Each submission is reviewed by at least three judges.

**When does judging happen?**  
Judging opens on **28 October**, immediately after submissions close. Results are announced on **6 November 2026, 16:00 UTC**.

**How is scoring broken down?**

| Criterion | Points | What Judges Look For |
|---|---:|---|
| Impact | 30 | Consistency with the challenge statements: how effectively the solution addresses a meaningful real-world problem for its intended users |
| Technical | 20 | How well the solution is built, including its technical implementation, reliability and architecture |
| Innovation | 15 | How original or creative the approach to solving the problem is |
| Demo | 15 | How clearly the demo shows the solution, its functionality, and the value it provides to its intended users |
| Product & UX | 10 | How intuitive, accessible, and easy to use the product is for its intended users |
| Sponsor Tech | 10 | How effectively the solution uses the sponsors' technology to address the problem and enhance the overall product |
| **Total** | **100** | |

**How are ties resolved?**  
On the Impact score, then on submission timestamp.

**What do judges actually reward?**  
A clear problem and user, agents that do real work rather than describe it, critique and recovery rather than a fixed pipeline, reliable execution, effective use of the sponsor technology, a usable output, and a demo that explains the solution clearly.

**What do judges discount?**  
UI polish without depth, single-agent systems presented as agent systems, hardcoded demo paths, broad concepts with weak implementation, and unsupported claims of value.

**Will I get feedback on my submission?**  
Yes. Judging starts immediately after submissions close, and results are announced on 6 November.
